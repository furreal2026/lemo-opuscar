# Colour Stick Explainer: changes from Stick-figure Explainer

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | one accent, no props filled | the world in flat colour; one power colour; red for limits | the user wants a colour film |
| §1 / §2 Figures | no additions to people | a transformed figure gains flat ears, tail, whiskers in the power colour | Meow Man must read as a cat-person at a glance |
| §3 Colour | paper, ink, greys, one red-orange accent | flat soft fills for props and sets, ginger = power, red = limit | colour without losing the one-meaning rule |
| §9 Pitfalls | second accent | power colour on props, loud fills, ears over the head | new risks the fills bring |
| §2 Materials | props are simple shapes | props in detailed line art: 6 px ink outline, 3 px inner detail lines (tiles, panes, handles, roof tiles, fringe, tape), flat fill | the user asked for objects as detailed as a reference drawing, in colour |
| §2 Materials (v2) | outlines are even strokes | every prop outline is a hand-inked ribbon: tapered ends, ±1 px wobble seeded from the shape itself (so moving props never boil), width varies along the line; more drawn detail per prop (fur tufts, wood grain, knots, turned legs, corrugated cardboard, chalk menus, speaker grills); the stick figures keep the clean even hose | the user wants props as detailed as hand-drawn comic explainers |
| §6 Camera (v2) | locked frames, pushes, pull-backs | each scene opens with a two-finger gesture borrowed from Tablet Sketch Explainer: a pinch from 1.06× to 1× and a 2° turn that settles to a slow 0.5° drift | the user asked for the tablet-style camera |
