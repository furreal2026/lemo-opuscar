## Description

One moldy slice of bread. Seven days. A body that slowly stops listening to you.

What if you turned into a zombie… but your mind stayed completely, painfully awake? You'd still remember your sister's name. You'd still know your best friend's laugh. You just couldn't tell them.

Inspired by the real Cordyceps fungus, which takes over the muscles of ants while leaving their brains almost untouched. (Don't worry: in real life it can't infect humans. Your body is too warm.)

🔬 The science
• Ophiocordyceps unilateralis infects carpenter ants. Its threads (hyphae) spread through the body and wrap the muscles, while the brain is mostly spared (Fredericksen et al., PNAS 2017).
• Infected ants climb, bite onto a leaf and hold on: the "death grip". A fungal stalk then grows from the ant's head.
• Human infection is fiction: at 37 °C our bodies are too hot for it.
